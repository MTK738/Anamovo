Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

Anamovo is built to create as little data as possible — and whatever it does create stays local. This page explains what the app processes and why.

Note: This policy describes Anamovo's planned functionality ahead of release and should be legally reviewed and finalized before launch.

1. Local by design

Anamovo does not transmit usage, exercise or health data to any server operated by us or by third parties. All data is processed and stored exclusively on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

2. What data is processed

  • Session history: the name, time, duration and cycle count of each completed exercise.
  • Custom exercises: breathing rhythms, titles and settings you create yourself.
  • Heart-rate data: if you connect an Apple Watch or AirPods Pro 3 and grant permission, the app reads your heart rate during a session via Apple HealthKit to display it to you.
  • Settings: your chosen color theme, reminder times, sound and haptic preferences.

3. Where it's stored

This data never leaves your device. If you have iCloud backup enabled in iOS system settings, Apple may include the app's data as part of your device's general backup — that happens through iOS itself, not through Anamovo.

4. Permissions

The app actively asks for permission before accessing health data (HealthKit) or notifications. You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS system settings without the app becoming unusable.

5. No tracking, no advertising

Anamovo contains no analytics software, no ad networks and no third-party SDKs that collect data. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

6. Your rights

Because all data lives on your device, you can view, edit or delete it at any time directly in the app — for example by removing individual sessions or uninstalling the app. For privacy questions, reach us via the support page.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects data processing, we will update this policy accordingly.
